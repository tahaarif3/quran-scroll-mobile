IqraLock — Terms of Use

Last updated: 13 September 2026

IqraLock is an iOS app that helps you build a Qur'an reading habit. By using the app you agree to these terms.

What the app does

IqraLock can use Apple's Screen Time / Family Controls APIs to shield apps you choose until you read. Shielding depends on Apple's system. IqraLock cannot guarantee that every selected app stays blocked in every iOS version or after you revoke permission.

Every current feature is available without paying.

Subscriptions

Optional subscriptions, if offered, are billed by Apple. Manage or cancel them in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. A 3-day free trial on the yearly plan converts to a paid yearly subscription unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

IqraLock never sees your card number. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Your responsibilities

  • You choose which apps to shield.
  • You are responsible for a Family PIN if you set one.
  • Bathroom breaks and unlock windows are temporary.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. Data stays on your device. There are no IqraLock accounts.
